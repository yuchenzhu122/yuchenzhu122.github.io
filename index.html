Job Market Paper

Predicting College Major Switching with Machine Learning

Machine LearningXGBoostSHAPPrediction Policy

Students in U.S. colleges can change their majors as they learn about their academic strengths and fit with different fields, but adjusting late is costly. Using administrative data, I examine what types of information predict major switching. Information available before college predicts switching only weakly. Prediction improves once students enroll and their records accumulate. Performance in a major’s core courses is key, predicting even switching that occurs several terms after the courses are taken. Routinely collected academic records can therefore identify switching risk well before the switch takes place, leaving time for advising to respond.
